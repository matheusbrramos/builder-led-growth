## pillar 1 · legibility

The machine can read, understand and use your product without ambiguity.

## pillar 2 · accessibility

The machine can get started without a human having to step in halfway through.

## pillar 3 · community

There is third-party material that future recommendations will feed on.

## pillar 4 · trust

The machine, and the human behind it, accept using it without reviewing every step.

## thesis limit

Builder-Led Growth decides who gets in; human economics decides who stays.

## builder is the pair

A builder is the pair: the person and the agent together.

## assisted decision

AI-assisted decision: the person chooses among options the machine assembled.

## delegated decision

Delegated decision: the person accepts or rejects a result the machine has already.
